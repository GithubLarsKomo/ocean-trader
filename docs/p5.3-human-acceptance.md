**Status:** ready after automated F.1 gate
**Scope:** `feat/p5.3-harbour-manoeuvring-core`
**Purpose:** final human and physical-phone acceptance before P5.3 can be considered merge-ready for `main`.

## Test routes

Standalone training:

- `/p5.html?scenario=alongside`
- `/p5.html?scenario=unberthing`
- `/p5.html?scenario=turning`

Campaign validation:

- start a campaign voyage with an arrival pending;
- open the 3D harbour through the existing campaign arrival action;
- verify that campaign arrivals always use **Alongside Berthing**, regardless of any `scenario` query parameter.

## Required devices / views

Minimum human evidence:

1. modern iPhone Safari, portrait;
2. same iPhone Safari, landscape;
3. desktop browser as a control/reference run.

Record device model, browser version and whether the session sustained visually smooth operation. The P5 target remains at least 30 FPS on a modern iPhone; obvious sustained stutter is a failure even if the manoeuvre can technically be completed.

## Human handling criteria

### H-01 — Telegraph and inertia

- FULL/HALF/SLOW AHEAD and ASTERN must not feel like direct velocity controls.
- Ahead → astern must visibly pass through propulsion response/reversal delay.
- A late engine order must not instantly rescue a badly planned approach.

**Pass:** the tester must plan speed changes ahead of the desired position.

### H-02 — Rudder and low-speed authority

- Rudder at STOP and near zero STW should have little effect.
- A short ahead kick should create useful rudder authority through propeller wash.
- MIDSHIPS must not instantly cancel existing rate of turn.
- Counter-rudder must visibly arrest rotation faster than MIDSHIPS alone.

### H-03 — Prop walk

- Astern prop walk must be noticeable on the Handysize without dominating every manoeuvre.
- Direction must remain consistent with the configured right-handed propeller.

### H-04 — Bow thruster

- Bow thruster must be useful at zero/very low speed.
- Effect must noticeably decay as STW rises.
- Hold control must release reliably on finger-up, cancellation, focus loss and app backgrounding.
- Multitouch use with rudder/telegraph must not accidentally release the active thruster finger.

### H-05 — Wind, current and instruments

- WND/CUR context must be visible before manoeuvring.
- HDG/COG and STW/SOG differences should be understandable during cross-current operation.
- Environmental drift must feel explainable by the displayed conditions rather than random.

## Scenario acceptance

### H-06 — Alongside Berthing

Goal: enter the berth window, become parallel and settle without excessive contact.

Pass conditions:

- successful completion requires deliberate speed reduction and alignment;
- a clean approach can be completed with zero damage;
- controlled fender contact at very low normal speed does not cause damage;
- hard lateral contact causes visible damage and does not settle immediately;
- the player must hold the safe berth envelope for about 3 seconds before completion.

Human question: **Did you have to think ahead about where the ship would be several seconds later?**

### H-07 — Unberthing

Goal: leave the alongside start, clear the quay, turn outbound and establish controlled motion through the exit gate.

Pass conditions:

- initial separation from the quay requires a meaningful combination of bow thruster, engine and/or prop walk;
- simply driving forward from the start does not immediately complete the scenario;
- wrong-heading passage through the exit region is rejected;
- successful completion feels distinct from Alongside Berthing.

### H-08 — Turning Basin

Goal: turn approximately 180° inside the basin and arrest the turn at low speed.

Pass conditions:

- the vessel cannot pivot unrealistically instantaneously;
- residual yaw must require anticipation/counter-action;
- reaching 180° with excessive speed or rate of turn is rejected;
- final success requires a controlled, arrested turn rather than merely crossing the target heading.

## UI / phone acceptance

### H-09 — Portrait

- no essential control is unreachable;
- engine orders, rudder and bow thruster remain usable without browser zoom;
- key instruments remain legible;
- scenario selector is usable in standalone training;
- no accidental page scrolling interferes with manoeuvring.

### H-10 — Landscape

- all primary controls remain visible and reachable;
- canvas area remains large enough to judge position and heading;
- multitouch helm + bow thruster operation is practical;
- no safe-area clipping around notch/home indicator.

## Campaign integrity

### H-11 — Settlement exactly once

Perform one campaign arrival through Alongside Berthing.

Pass:

- campaign cash/reputation/contract settlement happens once;
- final vessel condition reflects harbour damage;
- refresh/reopen after settlement does not settle again.

### H-12 — Interrupted manoeuvre

Start a campaign harbour manoeuvre, make some progress, then refresh or leave before success.

Pass:

- voyage is not settled;
- in-progress state restores without an active bow-thruster command;
- accumulated collision damage is retained;
- RESET cannot erase accumulated campaign damage.

### H-13 — Legacy fallback

Verify that the existing 2D harbour fallback remains reachable/functional until P5.3 human acceptance is formally complete.

## Acceptance record

Record each item as `PASS`, `FAIL` or `N/A`, with one sentence of evidence.

P5.3 is human-accepted only if:

- H-01 through H-12 are PASS;
- H-13 confirms the fallback remains intact;
- no blocker is found in physical-phone controls;
- the tester answers **yes** to: "Does the ship make me plan future motion rather than react at the last moment?"

Any failure should become a focused P5.3 follow-up issue/branch. Do not compensate for a failed handling criterion by weakening the automated AT-01…AT-10 gates.
