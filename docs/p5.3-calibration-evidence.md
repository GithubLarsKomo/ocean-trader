# P5.3-D — Vessel & Load Calibration Evidence

**Status:** calibration snapshot for P5.3-D  
**Reference vessel:** Handysize  
**Model:** deterministic P5.3 3-DOF gameplay model  
**Important:** these are simulation calibration coefficients and benchmark outputs, not manoeuvring data for a specific real vessel.

## Calibration intent

P5.3-D fixes two separate problems:

1. each class must be recognisably different under identical manoeuvring conditions;
2. low-speed yaw and prop-walk magnitudes must remain in a believable gameplay range rather than merely preserving a relative ordering.

The accepted qualitative hierarchy is:

`Coaster → Handysize → Feeder → Panamax`

with increasing inertia and stopping demand. Handysize is the reference low-speed vessel.

## Absolute low-speed correction

Before P5.3-D the provisional P5.3-B/C rudder coefficients could rotate the Handysize through several complete turns during a 20-second 4 kn hard-rudder benchmark. The model was deterministic but not credible for harbour handling.

P5.3-D reduces the normalized rudder-force coefficients by two orders of magnitude and reduces transverse prop-walk strength to an astern-dominant harbour-scale effect. Bow-thruster calibration from P5.3-C remains unchanged.

Reference targets after calibration:

- Handysize, 4 kn, HALF AHEAD, hard rudder, 20 s: about **29° heading change**;
- Handysize, astern prop-walk benchmark, 45 s: about **21° heading change**;
- Handysize, bow thruster at rest, 12 s: about **19° heading change**.

These are gameplay targets, not IMO trial criteria.

## Load sensitivity snapshot

AT-08 requires cargo state to alter response, stopping and turning for every class. The automated gate uses ratios rather than exact values so future parameter tuning can remain possible without losing the intended behaviour.

| Vessel | Empty/laden 20 s response distance | Laden/empty 6 kn stop distance | Empty/laden 4 kn heading change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Coaster | 1.50 × | 1.32 × | 1.98 × |
| Handysize | 1.72 × | 1.34 × | 2.47 × |
| Feeder | 1.88 × | 1.33 × | 2.77 × |
| Panamax | 2.51 × | 1.46 × | 3.50 × |

The automated minimums are deliberately below the snapshot values:

- empty response distance > laden response distance × 1.30;
- laden normalized stop distance > empty stop distance × 1.20;
- empty low-speed heading response > laden response × 1.15.

Handysize, as the reference class, has additional stronger margins.

## Benchmark definitions

### Harbour response

- initial state: STOP, zero vessel speed;
- command: FULL AHEAD;
- duration: 20 s;
- metrics: distance, final surge, final speed.

This measures command response rather than eventual terminal speed.

### Normalized crash stop

- initial speed: exactly 6 kn;
- initial machinery state: FULL AHEAD, shaft demand/actual = 1;
- command: FULL ASTERN;
- stop threshold: surge <= 0.05 m/s;
- metrics: stop time and travelled distance.

Using identical entry speed prevents class-specific acceleration from contaminating the stopping comparison while preserving mass, drag, engine response and reversal delay.

### Low-speed turn

- initial speed: exactly 4 kn;
- machinery: HALF AHEAD, shaft demand/actual = 0.6;
- command: hard starboard rudder;
- duration: 20 s;
- metrics: heading change, yaw rate and displacement.

### Prop walk

- starts from rest;
- astern command without rudder;
- verifies transverse stern force and correct yaw direction.

### Bow thruster

- starts from rest;
- full starboard bow thruster for 12 s;
- verifies visible lateral/yaw authority and remains subject to the P5.3-C speed cut-off.

## Acceptance conclusion

P5.3-D is acceptable when CI confirms:

- AT-07 class ordering for response, low-speed turn and normalized stopping;
- Panamax normalized laden stopping distance >= 1.8 × Coaster;
- Handysize remains between Coaster and Feeder in harbour response;
- AT-08 load sensitivity for every class;
- all earlier P5.3-A/B/C regression and persistence gates remain green.

The benchmark tests are authoritative. This document is the calibration snapshot used to explain why the accepted parameters were chosen.
