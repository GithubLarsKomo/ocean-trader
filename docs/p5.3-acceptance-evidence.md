## Remaining acceptance work

No further implementation slice is required before human testing.

Remaining gates are intentionally non-automated:

1. Handysize low-speed handling must be accepted by a human tester.
2. All three scenarios must be completed and judged distinct/useful in actual play.
3. Modern iPhone Safari must be checked in portrait and landscape.
4. Multitouch rudder / thruster / telegraph usability must be accepted on physical hardware.
5. Sustained visual performance should meet the >=30 FPS target without obvious long stutter.
6. One real campaign arrival must be checked for settlement exactly once, damage persistence and interrupted-manoeuvre behaviour.

Use `docs/p5.3-human-acceptance.md` as the binding checklist.
